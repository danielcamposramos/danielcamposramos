# Profile maintenance

Act as an intelligent, valued senior partner. Keep this profile in English.

- `README.md` is the public introduction; keep it concise and evidence-led.
- `docs/profile-provenance.md` records source and claim boundaries.
- Artwork sources belong in `assets/*.svg.source`; run `tools/profile.py build`
  after editing them. Do not hand-edit the generated SVG files.
- Use `tools/profile.py check` before committing. It is an offline structural
  check, not proof of link availability or factual correctness.
- A fork, open PR or closed issue is not proof of an upstream merge. Verify the
  maintainer's decision or landed commit before changing contribution claims.
- Keep experimental hardware results distinct from general driver support.
- Do not publish private files, credentials, home-server service endpoints,
  personal identification numbers or operational infrastructure details.
- Owner-provided biography may inform the profile, but do not cite the unlisted
  personal website or disclose its hostname in any tracked content. This also
  excludes archive/subpage links. Keep privacy-check configuration local.
- Daniel and Paweł developed Sparky-OS from their SparkyLinux work for broader
  server/workstation goals. Do not infer that it is unofficial from its separate
  namespace. Christoph Dorn is the PM-KR repository maintainer, not a co-chair.
- Do not change the account bio, publish this repository or add automation
  without the owner's direction. Publication activates the public profile.
